01 Who we are

Wrenchbook (the “App”) is provided by the Wrenchbook team (“we”, “us”, or “our”). This policy explains what information the App handles, where it is stored, and the choices you have.

For privacy questions or requests, use the App Support link on the Wrenchbook App Store product page.

This policy applies to the App and any features released under the same name, unless a separate policy is shown for a specific feature.

02 The short version

We do not operate an account system or a server that receives your content. Wrenchbook stores what you create on your device. We do not sell your information, show advertising, or use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Permissions are optional and controlled by iOS. You can delete notes in the App, revoke permissions in iOS Settings, or remove all App data by uninstalling the App.

  • No account, sign-in, email address, or phone number is required.
  • No advertising identifiers, cross-app tracking, or behavioral profiles.
  • No developer-operated analytics or crash-reporting service in the current version.
  • Your content is used to provide the App’s features, not to build a profile of you.

03 Information you create

Wrenchbook lets you create and store notes, lists, tags, attachments, settings, and photos you choose to add. This content is stored on your device and is not transmitted to us.

  • Notes and lists. Text, checklists, tags, and values you enter are stored in the App’s local database so the App can display, search, organize, and export them.
  • Photos. If you attach a photo, the App receives only the item you select through the system photo picker. The photo is stored with your note on your device. The App does not scan your photo library.
  • Settings. Preferences such as units, appearance, reminders, and App Lock are stored locally on your device.
  • Exports and shares. When you export or share content, iOS passes it to the destination you choose (for example, Files, Mail, or another app). That destination handles the content under its own privacy practices.
No cloud account. The current version does not create a developer-hosted copy of your content. If a future version adds optional sync, this policy and the App Store privacy answers will be updated before that feature is enabled.

04 Information we do not collect

In the current version, we do not collect or receive:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or postal address.
  • Contacts, calendars, or precise location.
  • Advertising identifiers or cross-app/website tracking data.
  • Payment card details (see “Purchases” below).
  • Your notes, attachments, or photos on a developer server.

We do not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs in the current version. Apple may provide crash reports and diagnostics to developers if you have enabled “Share with App Developers” in iOS Settings; we use those reports only to diagnose and fix problems. Apple handles that data under its own privacy policy.

05 Permissions

The App asks only for permissions needed by a feature you choose to use. You can change or revoke them at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security, or in the App’s settings.

PermissionWhy it is usedWhat leaves the device
Photos To attach a photo to a note you are creating or editing. The system picker gives the App only the item you select. Nothing. The selected image is stored with the note on your device.
Face ID / Touch ID To unlock the App when App Lock is enabled. Nothing. Biometric data stays with iOS; the App receives only a success or failure result.
Notifications To show local reminders you schedule. The App does not use a push server for these reminders. Nothing. Reminder content is scheduled locally on your device.
Camera or microphone Only if a feature you use offers capture. The App asks at the moment you use that feature. Nothing is uploaded by us. Captured content is stored with your note on your device.

The App does not request permissions it does not need. If you decline a permission, the related feature may be unavailable, but the rest of the App continues to work.

06 How information is used

Content you create is used on your device to:

  • Provide the App’s core features, including writing, organizing, search, and display.
  • Store your preferences and restore them the next time you open the App.
  • Create exports or shares when you ask for them.
  • Show local reminders when you enable them.
  • Protect the App with App Lock when you enable it.

We do not use your content for advertising, profiling, or sale. We do not combine it with data from other apps or websites.

07 Storage, backups, and retention

Your content is stored in the App’s container on your device. It remains there until you delete the content in the App or uninstall the App. Deleting a note removes it from the App’s active database; deleting the App removes its container, subject to the backup behavior described below.

Device backups

If you back up your device to iCloud or a computer, iOS may include App data in that backup according to your backup settings. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple (or your backup software), not by us. A deleted note may remain in an existing backup until that backup is replaced or expires.

No developer copy

We do not maintain a developer-hosted copy of your content in the current version, so there is no separate server retention period to expire. If this changes, we will update this policy and provide the relevant controls before the change takes effect.

08 Sharing and third parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. We do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

  • Apple system services. The App uses Apple frameworks for storage, photos, notifications, biometrics, and sharing. Apple processes those interactions under its own privacy policy.
  • App distribution. If you download the App from the App Store or test it through TestFlight, Apple may collect installation, purchase, and diagnostic data as part of those services.
  • Your exports. When you share or export content, the destination you choose receives it. Review that destination’s privacy practices before sharing.
  • Legal requirements. Because we do not hold a server copy of your content, we generally cannot produce it. If we receive a valid legal request for information we do hold (for example, support correspondence), we will respond only as required by applicable law and will notify you where lawful.

We do not include third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs in the current version. If that changes, this section will identify the provider and the purpose before the integration is enabled.

09 Purchases

If the App offers a paid feature or subscription, the purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple may provide us with a transaction identifier, product identifier, and anonymized sales reports so we can validate the purchase and understand aggregate sales.

The current version is free. If purchases are added later, this section and the App Store privacy answers will be updated before they go live.

10 Your choices and rights

You can control your information directly:

  • Edit or delete notes in the App at any time.
  • Export a copy of your content using the App’s export or share features.
  • Revoke permissions in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security.
  • Disable reminders or App Lock in the App’s settings.
  • Remove all App data by uninstalling the App (subject to device backups).

EEA, UK, and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability. Because the App is local-first, many of these requests can be fulfilled directly in the App. You may also contact us through the App Support link on the Wrenchbook App Store product page.

Where GDPR applies, we rely on the following legal bases: performance of a contract (providing the App’s features you request), consent (for optional permissions such as photos, notifications, or biometrics), and legitimate interests (security and troubleshooting). You can withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior processing.

California and other U.S. state privacy laws

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. If you are a California resident, you may have the right to know, delete, or correct personal information, and to non-discrimination for exercising those rights. Because we do not maintain a server copy of your content, most requests can be handled in the App. You may contact us through the App Support link on the Wrenchbook App Store product page.

Verification

If you contact us with a privacy request, we may ask for enough information to verify that the request relates to you. We will not ask for your App content, because we do not hold it.

11 Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK, where applicable). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App is local-first, we do not receive a child’s notes or photos.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us through the App Support link on the Wrenchbook App Store product page and we will take appropriate steps to delete it from any systems we control.

12 Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect the App and its data, including iOS’s app sandbox, device encryption, and optional App Lock with Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

  • Protect your device with a passcode and keep iOS up to date.
  • Use App Lock if you keep sensitive notes on a shared device.
  • Be careful when exporting or sharing content to third-party destinations.

We cannot guarantee the security of data stored on a device we do not control, or of content after you choose to share it with another app or service.

13 International users

The App stores content on your device. We do not transfer your content to a developer-operated server. If you use iCloud or another backup service, your data may be processed in the regions where that service operates, under that provider’s terms.

If you contact us, your correspondence may be processed in the country where we operate. We take reasonable steps to protect it consistent with this policy.

14 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the App, legal requirements, or our practices. The “Last updated” date at the top will change. If a change materially affects how your information is handled, we will provide a prominent notice in the App or on this page before the change takes effect.

Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the updated policy. If you do not agree, you can stop using the App and uninstall it.

15 Contact

We aim to respond to privacy requests within a reasonable period and within any period required by applicable law.
